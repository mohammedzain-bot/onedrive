Flawless Mirror Finish

Over time, your vehicle's clear coat accumulates micro-scratches, swirl marks, wash marring, and oxidation that dulls the original shine. Our professional paint correction and polishing service reverses these imperfections.

Using industry-leading compounds, specialized polishing pads, and dual-action polishers, our certified technicians safely level the clear coat to reveal the true depth and brilliant gloss of your vehicle's paintwork, prepping it perfectly for a Ceramic Coating or PPF application.

  • Swirl & Buffer Mark Removal
  • Advanced 3-Stage Compounding
  • Paint Depth Measurement
  • Deep Mirror Finish Restoration
